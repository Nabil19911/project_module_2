# UNIVERSITY OF EXCELLENCE - STUDENT CODE OF CONDUCT
Document ID: POL-SCOC-2024
Last Updated: December 2023

## 1. General Expectations
Students must treat staff and peers with respect. Harassment, bullying, or disruptive behavior in classrooms or digital forums is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Professionalism
Students are expected to communicate via official university email addresses. Using social media for official academic correspondence is discouraged.

## 3. Disciplinary Process
Violations of the code of conduct are investigated by the Student Discipline Committee. Sanctions may range from a formal reprimand to permanent exclusion from university premises.
