# UNIVERSITY OF EXCELLENCE - ATTENDANCE AND ENGAGEMENT POLICY
Document ID: POL-ATT-2024
Last Updated: January 2024

## 1. Minimum Requirements
Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 80% in all scheduled lectures and labs.

## 2. Warning Tiers
- Tier 1: At 85% attendance, the student receives a 'Soft Warning' email.
- Tier 2: At 75% attendance, a formal letter is sent to the student and the program lead.
- Tier 3: Below 70% attendance, the student may be flagged for academic withdrawal.

## 3. Valid Absences
Absences are considered valid only if supported by a medical note or an approved EC application.
