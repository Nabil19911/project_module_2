# UNIVERSITY OF EXCELLENCE - ASSESSMENT AND GRADING REGULATIONS
Document ID: POL-AG-2024
Last Updated: March 2024

## 1. Grading Scale
- 70% and Above: Distinction
- 60% to 69%: Merit
- 40% to 59%: Pass
- Below 40%: Fail

## 2. Resit Policies
Students who fail a module (score below 40%) are entitled to one resit attempt. However, the mark for any resit attempt is 'Capped' at the minimum pass mark (40%), regardless of the actual score achieved, unless the resit was granted due to an approved EC claim.

## 3. Weighting
Coursework typically accounts for 60% of the final grade, while the end-of-semester examination accounts for 40%.
